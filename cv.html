CV

Engineering geologist and GIS specialist with research and consultancy experience in Chile, Japan, Italy, Central America and South Asia, working on mountain hydrology, rain-induced hazards and geospatial tools for disaster risk reduction.

My research has combined long-term hydrological monitoring, field and drone surveys, physics-based modeling and remote sensing. In consultancy, I contributed to hazard mapping, landslide inventories and GIS training for government agencies in international cooperation projects, and to LiDAR-based slope-risk assessment of expressway networks in Japan.
